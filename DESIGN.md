# Digital Artist Studio
A calm, practical workspace for painters. Navigation: Atelier, Academy, Color Lab, Paint Catalog, Recipes, Library. One primary action per view. Lessons use short explanations, a practical exercise and source links. Avoid decorative dashboards, repeated cards and excessive motion. Use semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion and WCAG 2.2 AA as acceptance targets. Light and dark themes; neutral surfaces preserve color perception. Swatches always have text labels. Proposed typography: Manrope and DM Mono; verify font licenses before bundling. Proposed colors: graphite #181A1E, surface #25272C, copper #D69B65, canvas #F5F2EC, sage #718A78, slate #4B718D. Validate contrast per role before use.

## Product scope update
Free public access without accounts or inventory. Select commercial paints directly from the universal catalog; custom paint is an exception inside the mixer. Courses are original curated learning paths with authorized embedded videos, creator attribution, source links, original exercises and optional browser-local progress. No video rehosting, mandatory registration or paywall. This supersedes earlier inventory plans.
